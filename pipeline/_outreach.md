# Starter Pack Outreach Tracker — @hormuz-traffic.bsky.social

Goal: get listed in 5-10 Bluesky starter packs across OSINT, geopolitics,
maritime, and breaking-news niches. Each inclusion can drive up to 43%
of new follows in a single month for a small account.

## Outreach playbook (per pack)

1. Identify the curator's handle (column below).
2. Spend 2-3 days genuinely engaging with their recent posts (like + reply,
   on-topic, no filler).
3. Send a low-key DM:
   > Hi — I run @hormuz-traffic.bsky.social, daily Strait of Hormuz transit
   > data with news context. Would it fit your [PACK NAME] pack?
4. Update the row's `Status` and `Date` columns.

**Do NOT** send the DM before the engagement step. Cold asks get ignored
and burn the bridge.

## Status legend

- `new` — identified, no action yet
- `engaging` — actively replying/liking curator's posts
- `asked` — DM sent, awaiting response
- `added` — included in the pack 🎉
- `declined` — politely declined
- `cold` — no response after 14 days, give up gracefully
